# Urban commercial establishments expand from streets and ground across China in the digital era

This repository documents the data and scripts for the paper "Urban commercial establishments expand from streets and ground across China in the digital era". 

## Abstract
The digital era has catalyzed profound transformations in urban commercial spaces. However, research on the rise of “dark” and “implicit” shops—located outside traditional street-facing and ground-floor areas—lacks generalizability and fails to capture variations. This study investigates the spatial dynamics of commercial establishments across 287 Chinese cities from 2015 to 2023, using data from a local business platform Dazhong-Dianping. The findings reveal a widespread trend of establishments expanding horizontally within street blocks and vertically across building floors, excluding shopping centers. Notably, the proportion of non-street-facing and non-ground-floor shops continued to rise, accelerating in the post-COVID era despite declining shop numbers. Experiential consumption in entertainment, sports, and beauty industries—particularly board game cafés, yoga, and makeup studios—exhibited greater expansion. Higher socioeconomic tier cities experienced these transformations earlier and to a greater extent. These insights provide significant implications for mixed-use development, adaptive urban management, and retail innovations in the digital era.
