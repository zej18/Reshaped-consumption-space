# Urban commercial establishments expanding beyond streets and ground in China’s digital era!

This repository documents the data and scripts for the paper "Urban commercial establishments expanding beyond streets and ground in China’s digital era". 

## Abstract
As consumption gains importance, the spatial dynamics of commercial establishments in the digital era are reshaping urban structures and morphologies. This study examines shifts in establishments’ locations across 287 Chinese cities from 2015 to 2023, utilizing data from the local business platform Dazhong-Dianping. Results reveal a trend toward horizontal expansion within street blocks and vertical expansion to upper or lower building floors, reducing reliance on street-level locations. The rise in the proportion of non-street-facing and non-ground-floor shops outside shopping centers, particularly in experiential sectors like board game cafés and yoga studios, persisted and even accelerated after the COVID, despite a general decline in the overall shop numbers. This trend is consistent across city tiers, with more pronounced in cities with higher per capita GRP, more developed tertiary industries, and larger populations. These insights provide valuable implications for mixed-use development, adaptive urban management, and retail strategies in the digital era.
