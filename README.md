![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/96271afa-8a0a-4602-aaab-f8299a710044)# Expansion of urban commercial establishments from streets and ground in China’s digital era

This repository documents the data and scripts for the paper "Expansion of urban commercial establishments from streets and ground in China’s digital era". 

## Abstract
The digital era has catalyzed significant transformations in the spatial dynamics of commercial establishments. However, research on the rise of “dark” and “implicit” shops—those located outside traditional street-facing and ground-floor spaces—remains limited in scope and generalizability. This study investigates the spatial dynamics of commercial establishments across 287 Chinese cities from 2015 to 2023, leveraging data from the local business platform Dazhong-Dianping. The findings reveal a broad trend of establishments, outside shopping centers, expanding horizontally within street blocks and vertically across building floors. Notably, the proportion of non-street-facing and non-ground-floor businesses continued to rise, accelerating in the post-COVID era despite an overall decline in shop numbers. Experiential sectors in the entertainment, sports, and beauty industries—particularly board game cafés, yoga studios, and makeup studios—exhibited higher rates of expansion. Higher socioeconomic tier cities experienced these transformations earlier and more extensively. These insights offer valuable implications for mixed-use urban development, adaptive urban management, and retail innovations in the digital era.
